# Experiment 001 — index the gauge decision and link an in-stock cord

Recorded before the IndexNow submission. Status: defined, not yet run.

## Hypothesis

On 2026-09-26 a `site:cordgaugeguide.com` search returned no results, and three of four Amazon links did not resolve to an in-stock cord of the labeled gauge and length. Submitting the updated sitemap to IndexNow, and putting a checked in-stock cord next to the AWG pick, will get the URLs accepted by IndexNow. Within 14 days either a public site query will show at least one URL, or the click counter will record at least one outbound Amazon click.

## Audience

US shop and DIY buyers choosing a 120 V extension cord from nameplate amps and run length. The concrete job is a 15 A tool at 50 or 100 feet.

## Offer

A free calculator and chart. The purchase is on Amazon through tag `laqaer-20`. CordGauge earns a commission only if Associates attributes a qualifying order. The commission rate is not visible from this account, so fulfillment cost is treated as the existing hosting plans until a statement says otherwise.

## Channel

IndexNow (`api.indexnow.org`) for the live sitemap, plus internal links from the hub, chart, and tool guides. No paid ads. No email blast. No posts on personal social accounts.

## Cost ceiling

$0. Stop immediately if a step would require a paid API, an ad account, or a Vercel/Cloudflare upgrade.

## Success metric

- Required first: IndexNow responds 200 or 202 after the new pages are on `www.cordgaugeguide.com`.
- 14-day commercial signal: at least one counted click, or one indexed URL visible in a public site query.
- Profit signal: an Associates commission. That signal cannot be read until the owner enablement item is done. Absence of that report is not a failed experiment.

## Window

2026-09-26 through 2026-10-10.

## Stop or pivot

- If IndexNow rejects the batch, fix the key or host mismatch once. Do not keep resubmitting a rejected payload.
- If 2026-10-10 arrives with no index evidence and no clicks, do not add more tool pages. The next change is Search Console verification or a different in-stock product, not more articles.
- If either Southwire listing becomes unavailable or the page stops matching 12/3 and the labeled length, remove that link the same day.
